BEHIND THE MOVEMENT

Live arrivals.
Estimated journeys.

MTR publishes the next four arrivals at each station, not train GPS coordinates or unique train identifiers. The moving markers are illustrative estimates of trains approaching a station, not a count of the fleet.

Imminent arrivals are positioned on incoming segments using typical travel-time estimates. Speed adjusts smoothly to revised arrival times. On an estimated arrival, the station gently pulses and the marker pauses briefly before fading. This stop is illustrative, not a confirmed platform event. An amber dashed outline means MTR reports a delay on that station feed, not a confirmed delay for an individual train. Ambiguous branch approaches are omitted; markers are not persistent train identities.

We refresh the network approximately every 30 seconds while this tab is visible. Boards older than 90 seconds are excluded from the moving map. A missing marker does not mean no train is running.

All 10 heavy-rail lines are included. Light Rail and the High Speed Rail are outside this API. The map is a schematic, not a geographic or journey-planning map.
